# Progress timeline

A timeline tells the person what is done and what is left, one line per stage,
each starting with its status mark:

- `✓` done: what you did and what the person decided, in one short sentence,
  naming the real things you created or found. Link pull requests and issues.
- `●` now: the stage you are on.
- `◐` waiting on you: the person has to do something outside this session.
  Say what.
- `○` to do: what the stage does.
- `⊘` skipped: say why in a few words.

Use these marks, not emoji, as a list under the timeline's heading. Keep it
short, do not repeat details the person has just seen, and never put a token
or other secret in it.
